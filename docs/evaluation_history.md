# Evaluation history

The 30-city, 90-scene cohort was repeatedly inspected during development. Its city exclusion from gradient fitting does not make the final study a newly reserved confirmatory evaluation.

The retained `research/sub04_20260911/final_delivery_20260912/network_result.json` reports test comparisons dated 2026-09-12. Subsequent model development includes the four-head, compact-recovery and query-projection stages. The later development target record, `research/sub04_20260913/network_acceptance_20260913_revision.json`, sets accuracy/parameter targets while preserving the cohort's consumed status. The final prediction receipt explicitly identifies the cohort as previously consumed and records unequal additional training budgets relative to U-TAE.

These records establish that earlier test results were available during the refinement of the final architecture and training procedure. The selected checkpoints and new comparator/ablation checkpoints use validation criteria. The evidence does not establish independence of the overall development process from prior test feedback. Accordingly, the paper describes the scores as retrospective comparisons on cities excluded from parameter fitting, and the bootstrap intervals as paired uncertainty within that cohort. A newly reserved cohort would be needed for independent confirmation; collection of that cohort is outside this bounded manuscript revision.

The three final student lineages share teacher fields. Their score variation describes student training conditional on fixed teachers. Historical-input removal holds the trained networks fixed; natural local sparsity and removal of whole groups answer different questions and are reported separately.
