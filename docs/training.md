# Final model training

The manuscript's method section gives the teacher loss and structural transfers. [training_lineage.json](training_lineage.json) exports the exact configurations, parent paths, checkpoint hashes, and completion records for every retained stage of all three final models.

The [five-stage configuration table](configuration.md#final-system-training--section-iii-e) lists the optimizer, learning rates, effective batch, update budget, teacher weight and schedule for every stage. Validation considers 50, 10, 14, 26 and 8 raw/EMA candidates, respectively.

Block indices are zero based. Recovery retains bottleneck blocks 0/2/4 and encoder-level-2 blocks 0/1/3, then reindexes them. `compact_recovery/model.py` implements this tensor mapping. `compact_query_product/model.py` implements the final transfer and architecture. Every stage initializes a fresh optimizer and EMA from its selected parent; no optimizer history is transferred between stages.

The initial model and first refinement use history/emissivity dropout of 0.25. Later stages use zero dropout. Batch size is four. AdamW weight decay is 1e-4, maximum EMA decay is 0.995, and gradient norm is clipped to one. Learning rates are 1e-3 initially and 1e-4 for the next three stages. Final query refinement uses 5e-5 for inherited parameters and 5e-4 for new projections, with two microbatches of two. Warm-up lasts 300, 100, 100, 100, and 50 updates, respectively, followed by cosine decay; the final floor is 0.1 of the initial rate and preceding floors are 0.05. Earlier training uses automatic mixed precision; final query refinement uses FP32 with TF32 disabled. Full raw/EMA validation opportunities and selected weights are stored for every stage. Initial stages select with their recorded mixed-precision inference; compact recovery and final refinement use FP32 validation.

The loss is `(1-lambda) * scene_RMSE(prediction, reference, formal_mask) + lambda * scene_RMSE(prediction, teacher, observation_support)`, with 1e-6 inside each scene's square root. Scenes are sampled equally by region, then city, then acquisition. The spatial transforms rotate/reflect rasters and transform the wind-direction components in the context vector. Initial and final system stages use the full 160 by 160 scene; the matched scratch structural experiments use aligned 128 by 128 crops.

The first teacher is the equal average of eight inverse-transformed predictions from the initial seed-20260905 network. Its parameters are 0.75 times the validation-selected checkpoint plus 0.25 times the final EMA checkpoint, retaining selected buffers. The common interpolation coefficient was selected on validation. Each initial student receives this same teacher in its 1,000-update refinement. The second teacher is the equal average of eight views from each of those three refined historical networks. Both fields are generated once on all 603 training scenes, averaged in float64 and projected onto the supplied coarse means. Their producer scripts, exact parent hashes and cached-field hashes are included in the lineage record.

Historical U-TAE receives 18,000 supervised updates, the same 1,000-update first-teacher refinement, and 3,000 updates with the same second teacher. The retained full historical-fusion parent is evaluated at this same stage. For all three lineages, `revision_evidence/training_stage.json` verifies the completed budgets, shared optimization settings and teacher targets; both teacher stages also have identical stored scene and spatial-transform schedules. Initial training uses the same original training runner and sampling rule. Parent and U-TAE run records and teacher-stage schedules are included under their original relative paths.

At this matched stage, the full parent has 9,310,501 parameters and scores 0.427535665 K versus U-TAE's 0.481815815 K. The paired difference is 0.054280150 K, with a 95% city-bootstrap interval of [0.048920686, 0.059844633] K and improvement in 30/30 cities. Compact recovery and query refinement then produce 5,977,045 parameters and 0.426131996 K. The 35.8032% parameter reduction accompanies a 0.001403669 K RMSE reduction; its paired interval is [0.000977309, 0.001838765] K, with 25/30 city gains. The architecture and capacity differ between the full parent and U-TAE, so this stage comparison attributes neither a single module nor an isolated teacher effect.

The full-parent comparison reuses `sub04_20260913/final_confirmation/test` predictions. U-TAE checkpoints match those used in the final comparison. The two saved U-TAE prediction packets differ by at most 3.2553e-5 K on common finite support because of legacy numerical serialization/reprojection, with identical finite masks; the rescored macro RMSE differs by approximately 1.3e-9 K. The manuscript's displayed precision is unaffected.

Matched scratch structural runs use reference-only supervision, two paired seeds, 12,000 updates and identical 26-candidate validation schedules. Their difference from the final score combines initialization, supervision and optimization; it is not an estimate of distillation alone. Parent-to-compact inference speed was not measured under the current common timing boundary; the reported compression result concerns parameters and reconstruction accuracy.

Original training scripts and configurations preserve the executed development records, including their historical deadlines and source checks. Their runtime guards identify those original jobs; a new reproduction run needs new output paths and current job limits. Large data and checkpoint files are provided by their source identities and hashes rather than duplicated in the source archive. Preprocessing, normalization parameters, empty-slot conventions and city assignments are under `resources/historylst246/`.
